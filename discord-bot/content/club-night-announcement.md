This Thursday marks the kick-off of our 3rd Edition Warhammer 40,000 Escalation Event! It would be great to see as many of your lovely faces there as possible. More details are available in the dedicated #third-edition channel.

Not interested in 3rd Ed? Prefer rank-and-flank games, skirmish games, that copy of HeroQuest you've got collecting dust, or just want another opportunity to commit Heresy? No worries at all. Feel free to post below with what you're looking for a game of.

Grudge matches, pre-organised games, learning games, demos, and one-offs are all encouraged. If you're looking for a game, it helps to include the system, points level or game size, and roughly when you can start.

Games will usually start around 18:00. If you want to start earlier, please organise this with your opponent.
